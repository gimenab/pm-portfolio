Hi, I'm Gimena!

I work as a Project Manager. I have a technical background in Frontend Development, 5 years of experience in the industry and over two years as a PM, helping teams bridge the gap between technical complexity and seamless execution.

My main strength is combining rigorous attention to detail with empathetic leadership. I specialize in guiding multidisciplinary teams through complex transitions, bringing organization, patience, and clarity to every stage.

I am passionate about analyzing needs and optimizing resources to design structured, end-to-end action plans, always aimed at solving real problems.

Fundamentally, I value clarity, honesty, and continuous improvement. My goal is to foster a transparent and collaborative work environment that drives the team forward and ensures results are delivered.
